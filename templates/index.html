How QueueWise works

The application combines ride information and queue conditions to generate an estimated waiting time.

01

Choose your ride

Select the ride and enter the visit time, day type, and weather condition.

02

Enter queue details

Provide the number of people waiting, ride capacity, cycle duration, and other inputs.

03

View the estimate

The trained machine learning model predicts a waiting time based on its training data.

ⓘ About these predictions

QueueWise is a student project prototype. It does not measure the actual live queue or receive live visitor counts. Prediction quality depends on the training dataset and model.
